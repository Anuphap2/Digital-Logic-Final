# Google Stitch Design System

The Google Stitch Design System provides an immersive, tactile, and highly legible visual language specifically tuned for computer science, hardware, and digital electronics education.

## Core Pillars
1. **High-Clarity Light Canvas**: Crisp `#f8f9fe` background with pure white cards `#ffffff` and subtle borders `#e2e8f0`, avoiding warm yellowish tints.
2. **Electric Blue & Deep Tech Accents**: `#0066cc` as primary anchor with `#7c3aed` (Purple), `#10b981` (Emerald), and `#38bdf8` (Sky) accents.
3. **Clean Geometric Typography**: `Outfit` for modern geometric English headlines, `Prompt` for Thai clarity, and `JetBrains Mono` for Boolean formulas, truth tables, and pin labels.
4. **Interactive 3D Physicality**: Seamlessly integrates WebGL/Three.js interactive visualizations (DIP-14 IC package, K-Map Torus topology, and Moore FSM Water Tank) matching the exact same design language.
